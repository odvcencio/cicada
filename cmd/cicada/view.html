    Record

    Device setup and input monitoring. Use Live (L) for arming tracks and MIDI takes.

    PCM capture

    Choose the audio track and scene, arm the microphone, then record with one bar of count-in. Native capture requires duplex input. Stop publishes a retained take to the selected scene. Browser PCM remains available for recovery; timing is uncalibrated. Synth tracks provide accompaniment; audition recorded audio separately.

    idle · storage unchecked · timing unavailable · uncalibrated · monitor off

    Microphone processing settings appear after arming.

    Sampler audition

    Publish or recover a take to audition it. One voice; up to 8,388,608 source and output frames.

    Audio devices and input monitor

    Windows Studio uses WASAPI shared mode for capture and render. Input monitoring starts muted to prevent feedback; device buffers and levels update while playing.
